Tidy Tides

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Short version: Tidy Tides doesn't collect any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Tidy Tides has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising code.

What's stored on your device

Your coast, coins, helpers, sticker album, decorations, daily tasks and sound setting are saved only on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. This information is never sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes it.

Sharing postcards

If you tap to share a postcard of your beach, the picture is made on your device and handed to your phone's own share sheet. You choose where it goes; nothing is sent to us.

Purchases

Tidy Tides is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

The web version

The web version of Tidy Tides is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. The iPhone app doesn't connect to any server during play.

Children

Tidy Tides is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the Tidy Tides support page for how to reach us.
