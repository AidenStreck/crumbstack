Crumbstack

Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

Short version: Crumbstack doesn't collect any personal information. Your game progress stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Crumbstack has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and it doesn't include third-party analytics or advertising code.

What's stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, coins, boosters and sound settings are saved only on your own device so you can pick up where you left off. This information is never sent to us. Deleting the app (or clearing the website's data in your browser) deletes it.

In-app purchases

If you buy coins, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

The web version

The web version of Crumbstack is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service. See GitHub's Privacy Statement. The iPhone app doesn't connect to any server during play.

Children

Crumbstack is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? See the Crumbstack support page for how to reach us.
