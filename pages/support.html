Contact us

Found a bug or have an idea? Send us a message and we'll take a look.

How do I play?

Drag your finger to slide the plate. Each customer's ticket shows the burger they want, from the bottom bun up. Catch the glowing ingredient each time. Catching the wrong food or letting a customer run out of patience costs a heart.

How do I get more stars?

Serve customers quickly for bigger tips, serve orders back to back for a streak bonus, and finish with hearts to spare. Each level shows the points needed for each star before you start.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's website data for the web version, removes it. Progress doesn't move between devices.

A purchase didn't arrive or I want a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple. If coins didn't arrive, close and reopen the game first. For refunds, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Turning off music or sounds

Tap the speaker button on the home screen, or pause during a level, and use the Music and Sound effects switches.
