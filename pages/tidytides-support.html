How do I play?

Drag anywhere to walk your otter. Walk over trash to pick it up, drop it at the matching recycling machine, then carry what the machine makes to your shop stand. Tourists buy it for coins. Spend coins on the glowing pads to build machines, hire crab helpers and open new areas of the beach.

My bag says MAX

Your otter is carrying as much as it can. Drop things off at a machine or your shop stand, or upgrade your bag on the upgrade board.

What are Sea Stars?

When every area of a coast is open, you can set sail to the next coast. Sailing earns Sea Stars, which buy perks that last forever. Once you sail away you can't go back to the old coast, and the game warns you before you leave.

Do I earn coins while I'm away?

Yes, a little. Your helpers keep working for up to a couple of hours while the game is closed.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's website data for the web version, removes it. Progress doesn't move between devices.

Turning off sound

Tap the speaker button at the top of the screen.
